About PixelPress

PixelPress provides fast browser-based image compression, format conversion, resizing, and passport photo tools. Everything runs locally inside your browser tab.

Most online image utilities upload your photos to remote servers, queue them, and wrap them in heavy ads. With PixelPress, your files never leave your device — there is no upload endpoint, and closing the tab clears memory.

How it works

Images are decoded and re-encoded locally using the browser File API and HTML5 Canvas. HEIC files use an on-demand decoder loaded in the browser.

Format notes

JPG does not support transparency. Converting PNG to JPG fills transparent areas with white by design. Choose PNG if you need transparency.

How we fund the site

Display ads keep the tools free with no watermarks, no file limits, and no accounts.
