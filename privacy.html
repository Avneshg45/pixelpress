Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Your images

PixelPress has no upload endpoint. Files are read locally by your browser, processed in memory, and downloaded directly to your device.

2. Local storage

Only your theme preference (dark or light) is stored in localStorage.

3. Analytics and advertising

We use Google AdSense to serve ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or device identifiers to personalise ads. Learn more at Google Partner Sites Policy or manage preferences at adssettings.google.com.

4. Third-party assets

Fonts load from Google Fonts; optional HEIC and ZIP libraries load from jsDelivr.

5. Children's privacy

PixelPress does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
