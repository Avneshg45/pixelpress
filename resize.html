Common size targets

  • Instagram post — 1080×1350 px
  • Story / Reel cover — 1080×1920 px
  • YouTube thumbnail — 1280×720 px
  • Social share card — 1200×630 px
  • Passport 35×45 mm — 3.5×4.5 cm at 300 DPI

Which unit should you pick for print?

Use cm or mm with 300 DPI for print output. At 300 DPI, 3.5 cm equals 413 px, whereas at 72 DPI it is only 99 px.

Upscaling quality

High-quality canvas smoothing works well up to ~2×. Larger upscales will show softness limited by the source image.
