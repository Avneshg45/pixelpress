Frequently asked

Are my images really not uploaded?

Correct. There is no upload endpoint. The page reads the file with the browser's File API, draws it to a canvas and re-encodes it locally. Verify in DevTools → Network while a job runs.

Is there a file-size limit?

80 MB per file. Beyond that it's your device's memory, not us.

Does compressing hurt quality?

Any lossy encoder trades quality for size. Use the quality slider, or WebP at 80%+ for a near-invisible result at a fraction of the size.

Why HEIC?

iPhones save HEIC by default and many websites reject it. The converter decodes HEIC in-browser and outputs a standard JPG.

Is it free for commercial work?

Yes. Output files are yours with no attribution required.
